Lovestruck Books Presents: Grim Tidings Release-Day Bash with B.K. Borison

Arts at the Armory · Tue, Sep 15 · 6:30pm

A new whimsical, haunting romance. When a runaway Grim Reaper tries to save a life, she forms an unlikely partnership with a Guardian Angel.

Celebrate release day with New York Times bestselling author B.K. Borison as we gather at Arts at the Armory in Somerville for a special conversation about Grim Tidings.

In conversation with New York Times bestselling author, Ashley Poston, B.K. will discuss her enchanting new romance that blends magic, mischief, and irresistible chemistry.

When runaway Grim Reaper Darcy Moore decides to save a life instead of claiming it, she finds herself at odds with Guardian Angel Gabriel Aetos, a stubborn protector with plenty of reasons not to trust her. As sparks fly between these unlikely rivals and the Department of Hauntings & Spirits closes in, Darcy and Gabriel must navigate fate, second chances, and feelings that refuse to stay buried.

Join us for an evening of behind-the-scenes stories, author insights, and all the swoony, spooky fun of Borison’s latest enemies-to-lovers romance, followed by a meet and greet in celebration of the release.

Arts at the Armory serves delicious locally sourced food and drink at the Performance Hall bar when doors open (usually one hour before the show).
