Boogie Nights

Amherst Cinema · Tue, Sep 15 · 7pm

In 1977 Los Angeles, idealistic porn producer Jack Horner (Burt Reynolds) aspires to elevate his craft to an art form. Horner discovers Eddie Adams (Mark Wahlberg), a hot young talent working as a busboy in a nightclub, and welcomes him into the extended family of movie-makers, misfits, and hangers-on that are always around.

Adams’ rise from nobody to a celebrity adult entertainer is meteoric, and soon the whole world seems to know his alter ego, “Dirk Diggler.” Now, when disco and drugs are in vogue, fashion is in flux and the party never seems to stop, Adams’ dreams of turning sex into stardom are about to collide with cold, hard reality.

BOOGIE NIGHTS features an amazing, award-winning supporting cast, including John C. Reilly, Heather Graham, Julianne Moore, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Don Cheadle, William H. Macy, and many more.

Presented in a 4K restoration.

Directed by Paul Thomas Anderson · R
