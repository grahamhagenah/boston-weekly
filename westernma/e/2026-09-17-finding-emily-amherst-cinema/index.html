Finding Emily

Amherst Cinema · Thu, Sep 17 · 2:10pm, 9:40pm

When a lovesick musician is given the wrong number for his dream girl, he teams up with a driven psychology student to find her.

Together, they spark a hilarious campus-wide frenzy that tests their own hearts and ambitions along the way.

“Welcome back the British rom-com! It’s been a while since we’ve had a profoundly lovely film that swaggers into life from the opening disco beats (New Order’s Blue Monday) and subsequently demonstrates an unwavering surety of purpose.”

Directed by Alicia MacDonald · PG-13
