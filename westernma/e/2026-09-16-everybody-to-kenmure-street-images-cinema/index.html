Everybody to Kenmure Street

Images Cinema · Wed, Sep 16 · 7pm

Presented as part of Civic Cinema, our biennial series on democracy and civic engagement. This year's films focus on journalism/freedom of the press, as well as stories of collective action. Each screening will be paired with a talk with local leaders, community organizers, and academics.

Our 2026 edition of this series is presented with generous support from The Ginny Fund, a grant organized by the Friends of the Milne Library.

Join us for a social hour before the movie in the lounge! From 6pm-7pm meet and mingle with grassroots organizers and community leaders.

In May 2021, a UK Home Office dawn raid triggers one of the most spontaneous and successful acts of civil resistance in recent memory. In Pollokshields, Scotland’s most diverse neighbourhood, hundreds of residents rush to the streets to stop the deportation of their neighbours.

Directed by Felipe Bustos Sierra · 1h 38m
